The problem

Adherence is a distribution problem before it is a clinical one

Members with several chronic conditions fill many prescriptions across many refill dates. Getting to the pharmacy, affording the copay, and keeping five refill schedules straight are ordinary obstacles with expensive consequences — gaps in therapy, avoidable utilization, and adherence measures that drag on plan performance.

Mail order removes the trip but adds days, and it cannot respond when something goes wrong. What moves the numbers in a dense urban market is a pharmacy close enough to deliver the same day and organized enough to keep members on schedule without being asked.

How we help

Built around the measures you are held to

Every one of these starts with the medication actually reaching the member.

  • Adherence by design — automatic refills and medication synchronization keep members covered rather than relying on them to reorder.
  • Access without the trip — delivered to the member's door, usually within 24 hours, by our own team.
  • Every setting your members live in — at home, in assisted living, and in long-term care.
  • A member experience that reflects the membership — bilingual service and multi-language labeling as standard.
  • Affordability addressed early — lower-cost equivalents discussed with the prescriber before a member abandons therapy.
  • Density, not distance — more than one million prescriptions a year, all of it in three counties.
