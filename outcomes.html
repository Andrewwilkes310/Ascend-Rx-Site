Example provider group results

Same patients, same market — different pharmacy

A South Florida Medicare Advantage provider group compared two groups of its own Medicare patients over a three-month period in 2024: those filling with Ascend Rx, and those using outside pharmacies. The patients served by Ascend Rx used substantially less acute care.

Metric Ascend Rx patients Outside pharmacy patients Difference
Hospital admissions per 1,000 47228↓ 79%
Emergency room visits per 1,000 140410↓ 66%
Readmission rate 10%28%↓ 18 pts
↓79% Fewer hospital admissions per 1,000 patients
↓66% Fewer emergency room visits per 1,000 patients
10% Readmission rate, against 28% for patients using outside pharmacies

About this analysis. This is an observational comparison of two groups of Medicare patients within a single provider group over a three-month period in 2024. Patients were not randomly assigned, and the groups were not matched for baseline health status, so the results show a strong association rather than proving causation. We are happy to walk partners through the methodology and the underlying data in detail.
