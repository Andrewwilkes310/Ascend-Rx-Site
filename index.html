A trusted local pharmacy partner

The scale of a national pharmacy. The reach of a local one.

Ascend Rx delivers one million prescriptions a year to seniors at home and in long-term care facilities, and actively cares for more than 25,000 Medicare, Medicaid, and dual-eligible Medicare Advantage patients across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach. A national mail-order pharmacy cannot put a person at a patient's door this afternoon. We can, and we have been doing it here for more than fifteen years.

More about Ascend Rx
How we do it

A closed-door pharmacy built for accuracy at volume

Our Doral pharmacy is purpose-built for high-volume dispensing, with verification at every step of the fill, cold-chain handling for temperature-sensitive medications, and a licensed pharmacist reviewing every order before it leaves the building.

Verified at every step

Each prescription is checked through the fill process and reviewed by a licensed pharmacist before it ships.

Delivered by our own team

Not dropped in the mail. Our drivers deliver across all three counties, same day and next day.

Refills that arrive before they run out

Chronic medications refill automatically and synchronize onto a single delivery day.

A pharmacist on the phone

Patients, nurses, and prescribers reach a clinical team member, not a queue.

Adherence data back to the care team

Real-time medication and adherence feedback to physicians and care coordinators, with formulary alternatives.

Packaging built for adherence

Compliance packaging by dose, day, and time for patients managing many medications.

Bilingual by default, not by request
