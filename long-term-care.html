The problem

Medication administration is where the day goes wrong

A resident is admitted in the afternoon and their medications are somewhere else. A new order comes in after hours. A cycle arrives short. A bubble pack is mislabeled and a nurse has to stop and sort it out. Every one of these is a call to the pharmacy, and every call is time your staff does not have.

When the pharmacy is hours away or answers through a national call center, small problems become long ones. What a facility actually needs is a pharmacy close enough to fix things the same day, and organized enough that most of them never happen.

How we help

A local pharmacy partner built around your medication pass

Ascend Rx supports assisted living communities, group homes, long-term care facilities, and hospice across South Florida. We deliver scheduled cycle fills and same-day orders with our own drivers, package medications by dose, day, and time so administration is straightforward, and give your nursing team a pharmacist they can reach directly.

What changes for your residents

Medications that arrive right, and on time

  • Clearer administration — packaged by dose, day, and time rather than by bottle.
  • Faster starts on admission — new residents can begin therapy the day they arrive.
  • Fewer gaps — cycle fills and refills managed on a schedule, not by request.
  • Care that follows the resident — the same pharmacy at home, in assisted living, and in hospice.
