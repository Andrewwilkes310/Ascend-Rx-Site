The problem

The prescription is the easy part

A patient leaves your office with a plan. Then the plan meets the rest of their week: a pharmacy across town, no ride, a line, a price surprise, a medication that needs prior authorization, four other prescriptions that run out on four different days. Some of them never start the therapy at all. Many stop within ninety days.

Your staff absorbs the fallout — refill calls, faxes, authorization paperwork, and patients who arrive at the next visit having taken something other than what you prescribed. And the measures your organization is judged on move quietly in the wrong direction, months before anyone sees a report.

How we help

A pharmacy that closes the gap between the visit and the medication

Ascend Rx takes responsibility for what happens after the prescription is sent. We fill it, a pharmacist reviews it, and our own driver brings it to the patient's door — usually within 24 hours. Refills continue automatically, synchronized so the patient receives one delivery instead of five trips. If something needs prior authorization, our team handles the paperwork.

What changes for your patients

Fewer reasons to stop taking their medications

  • Therapy starts sooner — medication arrives at the door instead of waiting for a trip to the pharmacy.
  • Refills don't lapse — chronic medications refill automatically before the patient runs out.
  • One delivery, not five errands — medications synchronize onto a single day.
  • They understand the instructions — labels and conversations in English or Spanish.
What changes for your practice

Less work, not more

Referring to Ascend Rx should reduce your team's workload from the first week, not create a new integration project.

Fewer inbound refill calls

Automatic refills and a direct patient line move routine refill traffic off your front desk.

Prior authorizations handled

Our team works the paperwork and follows up, then tells you when it clears.

A named pharmacist to call

A clinical contact who knows your practice, reachable during business hours.

Cost conversations, handled

When a medication is unaffordable, we flag it and discuss lower-cost equivalents with you rather than leaving the patient to abandon it.

Simple referral

Send the prescription the way you already do. Secure fax and e-prescribing both work.

Adherence data back to your team

Real-time medication and adherence feedback to your PCPs and care coordinators, including formulary alternatives — integrated with your EMR where supported.

Getting started

From first conversation to first fills

Onboarding is deliberately light on your team. Most of the work is ours.

  1. Agreements in place

    We execute a BAA and NDA so patient data can move safely between us.

  2. Share your data

    Send a claims extract and your provider roster. Nothing else is needed from your staff.

  3. We review and report back

    We analyze the population and show you where the medication-related gaps and costs are.

  4. Pilot and expand

    Start with a few centers, set a standing check-in, then extend once it is working.
